About Nansei

Rooted in the land.

Nansei Organics grows, mills and packs organic rice, dried flowers, beverages and flours on our certified farm in Tamil Nadu — and sends them straight to your kitchen.

(01) Where we begin

A small farm. A big standard.

We believe food should be traceable. Every grain, flower and flour we sell comes from our own fields and our own mill, so we can tell you exactly where it grew and how it was made.

We grow heritage varieties because they are worth keeping — and we mill them slowly, by hand, because heat and speed take away what makes them good.

When you order, your parcel is packed by the same small team that handled it at harvest. And if you have a question, you talk to us — not a call centre.

Hand-milled

Packed & sent

Packed by hand in small batches and handed to a tracked courier for your door.
