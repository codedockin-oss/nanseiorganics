How we mill

Milled by hand on a wooden thirugai

Before rice mills, every village husked its paddy on a thirugai: two heavy wooden discs turned by hand. We still mill this way, a little at a time, the way Nammalvar encouraged farmers to.

  1. 01

    Turned by hand

    No motor. Someone sits at the mill and turns it, so each batch is small.

  2. 02

    Husk off, bran kept

    The wooden discs crack the husk without polishing the grain, so the rice stays unpolished.

  3. 03

    Packed at the farm

    Milled rice is cleaned, packed and sent from Achankulam.

Meet the Farmer

Rooted in the
Land He Loves

“Farming isn't just our livelihood — it's our language, our prayer, our way of speaking to the earth.”

has tended the same fields his grandfather once walked, nurturing heirloom paddy varieties that most of the world has forgotten. Guided by the seasonal rhythms of the land, he farms without chemical inputs choosing instead the wisdom of composting, water conservation, and deep ecological attentiveness.

His commitment to the wooden hand mill is not nostalgia it is conviction. He believes every revolution of that ancient millstone locks in something no industrial machine can replicate: the living integrity of the grain, the warmth of unhurried craft, and an unbroken chain of knowledge passed from one generation to the next.

Questions

Good to know

Delivery, returns and payment, in short. For anything else, message us on WhatsApp.

Where do you ship from, and where do you deliver?

Every order is packed at our farm in Achankulam, near Srivilliputhur in Tamil Nadu, and sent by courier to addresses across India. Delivery is free on orders above ₹999.

How do I track my order?

Sign in and open My Orders. Once your parcel is dispatched you will see the courier, the AWB number and a tracking link there.

What if something arrives damaged or wrong?

Tell us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a few photos or a short video of the product and packaging. We reply within 1–2 working days and send a replacement or refund. The full terms are on the refund policy page.

How can I pay?

UPI, debit and credit cards and net banking through Razorpay, or cash on delivery. Online refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, until it is dispatched. Open My Orders and choose “Cancel order”, or message us with the order number.
