Traditional Knowledge

The Traditional Hand Mill

Discover the story behind our grains. Explore the traditions, wisdom, and natural farming practices that make our slow stone-milled grains unique. Operating at low RPM with zero friction heat, our hand-operated mill preserves every active nutrient.

Thirugai Milling

Authentic, Zero-Heat Stone Milling

Traditional stone hand-milling is slow and manual. It generates no heat damage, protecting valuable enzymes, iron, calcium, and vitamin contents that are completely lost in modern high-RPM industrial mills.

  1. 01

    Stone Ground Integrity

    Slow grinding ensures no heat damage and preserves original dietary fibers and minerals.

  2. 02

    Artisanal Hand Operation

    Zero electrical power is used. Every single bag represents our dedication to organic heritage.

  3. 03

    Direct Farm Sourcing

    Directly trace your rice back to the fertile soils of our certified 25-acre organic farm.
