Pichai Murugan

Meet the Farmer

Rooted in the
Land He Loves

"Farming isn't just our livelihood — it's our language, our prayer, our way of speaking to the earth."

Pichai Murugan has tended the same fields his grandfather once walked, nurturing heirloom paddy varieties that most of the world has forgotten. Guided by the seasonal rhythms of the land, he farms without chemical inputs — choosing instead the wisdom of composting, water conservation, and deep ecological attentiveness.

His commitment to the wooden hand mill is not nostalgia — it is conviction. He believes that every revolution of that ancient millstone locks in something that no industrial machine can replicate: the living integrity of the grain, the warmth of unhurried craft, and the unbroken chain of knowledge passed from one generation to the next.

Inspired by the teachings of agrarian philosopher Nammalvar, Pichai has become a quiet guardian of traditional rice cultivation in Tamil Nadu — growing not just food, but a legacy that can be tasted in every bowl.
