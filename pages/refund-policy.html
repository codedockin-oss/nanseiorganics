Returns & refunds

If something is not right with your order, we will make it right — quickly and without hassle.

1. Return Eligibility

You may request a return or replacement within 7 days of delivery if:

  • The product arrived damaged or broken
  • You received the wrong product or wrong quantity
  • The product is defective or of significantly poor quality
  • The product is past its expiry date at the time of delivery
  • The package was tampered with or seal was broken on delivery

Not Eligible for Return

  • Products returned after 7 days of delivery
  • Products that have been opened, used, or partially consumed (unless defective)
  • Products damaged due to improper storage or handling by the customer
  • Change of mind or personal preference (we encourage you to read product descriptions carefully)
  • Orders where incorrect address was provided by the customer

2. How to Initiate a Return

1

Contact us within 7 days of delivery
Email us at or WhatsApp us at with your order number and reason for return.

2

Share photos or a video
Attach clear photos or a short video of the damaged/wrong product and the packaging. This helps us process your request faster.

3

We review your request
Our team will review your request within 1–2 business days and confirm whether it is approved.

4

Return pickup or drop-off
For approved returns, we will arrange a free pickup from your address or guide you to drop off the package at the nearest courier point.

5

Refund or replacement processed
Once we receive and inspect the returned product, your refund or replacement will be processed within 5–7 business days.

3. Refund Methods

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at the time of purchase:

  • Razorpay (UPI / Card / Net Banking): Refunded to the original payment source within 5–7 business days
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Refunded via bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) — you will need to provide your bank account details
  • Wallet / EMI: Refunded to the respective wallet or as per the payment provider's policy

You will receive an email or SMS confirmation once the refund is initiated. Bank processing times may vary.

4. Order Cancellations

Before Dispatch

You can cancel your order any time before it is dispatched. Go to My Orders on our website and click Cancel Order, or contact us directly. A full refund will be issued within 5–7 business days.

After Dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled. You may refuse delivery at the door, and we will process a refund after the product is returned to us. Note: COD orders refused at delivery may incur a return shipping fee.

5. Damaged in Transit

If your order arrives visibly damaged (crushed box, broken seal, leaking product), please:

  • Take a photo or video before opening the package
  • Do not discard the packaging — it may be needed for the courier claim
  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with the evidence

We will arrange a free replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

6. Refund Timeline

Day 0 — Return Request SubmittedYou contact us with your order number and reason
Day 1–2 — Request ReviewedOur team reviews and approves/rejects the return
Day 3–5 — Product Picked UpCourier collects the product from your address
Day 5–7 — Refund InitiatedRefund processed to your original payment method
Day 7–10 — Refund ReceivedAmount reflects in your bank/wallet (depends on your bank)

7. Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct product exchanges. If you received the wrong product, we will send the correct one after collecting the wrong item. If you wish to order a different product, please place a new order after your refund is processed.
